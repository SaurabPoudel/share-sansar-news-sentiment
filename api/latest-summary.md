## Things a Retail Investor Might Not Know

1. NEPSE operates Sunday through Thursday. Friday and Saturday are market holidays in Nepal.
2. A stock hitting the ±10% circuit limit means trading is frozen at that price — no more orders can be executed beyond that level for the day.
3. Book closure date is the cutoff for receiving declared dividends or bonus shares. You must hold shares BEFORE the book closure date to be eligible.
4. T+2 settlement means shares bought today are credited to your demat account 2 business days later. You cannot sell shares you just bought until settlement.
5. When a company announces a bonus share, the stock price adjusts downward proportionally on the ex-date. A 10% bonus on a Rs.1000 stock adjusts to ~Rs.909.
